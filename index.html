Privacy Policy — Imperium Invictum

Last updated: July 2026

Imperium Invictum collects no personal data. It is a single-player, offline strategy game. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics.

What the app stores

Your campaign saves and settings (audio volume, difficulty, UI scale) are stored only on your own device using the browser/WebView's local storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Data we collect and share

  • Personal data collected: none.
  • Data shared with third parties: none.
  • Advertising / tracking: none. The game contains no ad SDKs and no analytics SDKs.
  • Network use: the game runs entirely from assets bundled in the app and does not require an internet connection to play. It does not send your data anywhere.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
